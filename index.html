由来

YURAI · WHERE THIS CAME FROM

行灯 Andon were the paper-and-wood lanterns that lit Edo-period rooms and doorways — a small oil wick glowing behind a washi paper panel. This light borrows that idea instead of a bulb icon: a lantern you actually light by hand.

Pull the red cord to light it. Choose how it burns below. Set the oil level to bring the flame up or down.
